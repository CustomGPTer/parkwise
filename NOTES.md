First release of Parkwise: save where you parked with one tap and walk straight back to it.

- Saves as soon as the GPS is within 30 ft, then fine-tunes; a rough save after 10 seconds underground.
- Find my car: live arrow, distance, walking time, direction pulses, arrival buzz, camera view, Google Maps and Waze.
- Car parks: level, bay and zone colour, read from a photo of the sign on the phone.
- Parking ticket countdown with reminders 15 and 5 minutes before it runs out, cost and ticket photo.
- Parked notification, three widgets, a Quick Settings tile and shortcuts.
- History, stats, places, sharing by message or QR code, offline maps.
- Optional: auto-save by car Bluetooth or motion, several vehicles, app lock, larger text, high contrast, encrypted backup.

Android 8 or later. Free, no adverts, no account. Everything stays on your phone.
