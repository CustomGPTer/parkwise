What Parkwise does not do

Parkwise has no live location sharing with friends and no turn-by-turn walking route of its own: for a route it hands over to Google Maps. The map pictures need the internet unless you save the area first. It is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself.

Where the other app does more

Find parked car - Parking spot is on Google Play and updates itself, and it has a few small games built in.
