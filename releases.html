Release notes

What changed in each version of Parkwise. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 23 November 2026Latest

  • First release. Save where you parked with one tap and walk straight back to it.
  • Saves as soon as the GPS is within 30 ft, then fine-tunes for 30 seconds. Underground it saves its best reading after 10 seconds. Move the pin by hand, with satellite view.
  • Find my car: the map opens on you and the car, an animated route line, a live arrow, distance, walking time, direction pulses and an arrival buzz. Camera view, Google Maps and Waze.
  • Car parks: level, bay and zone colour, and reading the sign from a photo on the phone.
  • Parking ticket: countdown, reminders 15 and 5 minutes before and when it runs out, the cost and a photo of the ticket.
  • A parked notification, three widgets, a Quick Settings tile and icon shortcuts.
  • History by day, monthly costs, stats, places named for you, sharing by message or QR code, offline maps.
  • Optional extras, off until switched on: auto-save by car Bluetooth or when you stop driving, several vehicles, app lock, larger text and high contrast.
  • An encrypted backup file, kept on your phone or in Google Drive, and automatic backup.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called Parkwise.apk. It is 42.0 MB (42,003,316 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

16f4d7d51050272b5f8b6e44cd93cc6eb6cceb98b3a315d2a089b8a35f47879b

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Parkwise.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
